Rural Heritage & Pride

Rooted in Tradition, Rising with Technology

Sundarpur is an award-winning model Digital Smart Gram Panchayat. Nestled amidst lush green agricultural landscapes along sacred river tributaries, our village represents the perfect harmony of rich Indian cultural heritage, Vedic values, and state-of-the-art 21st-century digital empowerment.

Historical Heritage

Founded in the mid-19th century by agricultural pioneers and master craftsmen, Sundarpur flourished as a peaceful trading village renowned for its mango orchards and stepwells. The ancient Someshwar Mahadev Temple stands as a testament to centuries of spiritual unity and devotion.

Modern Digital Transformation

In 2023, the Gram Sabha unanimously embraced sustainable modernization. Today, 100% of street lighting is powered by solar micro-grids, biometric kiosks enable doorstep pension disbursements, and every child studies with digital curriculum tools.

Traditions & Celebrations

Our Rich Culture & Sacred Festivals

Celebrated with vibrant joy, communal harmony, traditional music, and colorful festivities

Phalguna Purnima

Holi – Festival of Colors

Celebrated with natural herbal gulal prepared from village marigolds and palash flowers. Villagers gather at the central Chaupal for folk singing (Chaiti & Phagua), sweets distribution, and joyful greetings.

Kartika Amavasya

Diwali – Festival of Lights

Over 20,000 earthen clay diyas handcrafted by local potters illuminate every courtyard, pond bank, and farm bund. Prayers are offered to Goddess Lakshmi for agrarian prosperity and peace.

Kartika Shukla Shashthi

Chhath Puja – Vedic Sun Worship

The supreme Vedic festival worshipping Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya. Thousands gather at the cleaned village river ghats for evening and dawn arghya with traditional bamboo soop, thekua, and fruits.

15th August

Independence Day

Solemn flag hoisting at Panchayat Bhawan followed by patriotic parades by school children, tribute to village army veterans, student debates, and distribution of laddu sweets.

26th January

Republic Day

Annual Special Gram Sabha where the entire village discusses social harmony and budget allocations, honors academic toppers, and pledges commitment to democratic unity.

Annual Mela

Gram Devi Mela & Kisan Fair

Our vibrant 3-day annual festival featuring traditional wrestling (Dangal), agricultural innovation showcases, folk dance drama (Nautanki), and pottery fairs bringing families together.
